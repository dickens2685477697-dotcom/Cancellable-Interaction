# Study 1 analysis

Events are matched in recorded order: enter → hold_complete → success; early exits and interruptions terminate attempts. Unmatched and incomplete attempts are recorded in quality_summary.json. Trial numbers are logical successful-trial numbers, not segment or case numbers.

All values are unfiltered descriptive summaries, with no outlier removal. Entry is the minimum signed rate of change of speed in the first 0.2 s after entry, restricted to inside samples with valid logged acceleration. Hold is mean/median/95th percentile logged speed from 0.5 s after entry until the hold-complete event, restricted to inside samples. Withdrawal is logged speed in the first 0.3 s after success, truncated at the next entry or end of recording. The signal-to-exit interval includes response and motion; it is not pure reaction time.

Joint speeds are displacement divided by elapsed time between adjacent valid positions in the same source, tracking epoch and reference-space epoch, with 0 < interval ≤ 50 ms. Blank values indicate unavailable measurements, not zeros. These fixed windows are exploratory and do not reproduce a published segmentation method. Time is in seconds, speed in m/s, acceleration in m/s². Logged acceleration is the signed derivative of speed, not the magnitude of vector acceleration. Sample counts and window durations are provided for interpretation. Reference epochs absent from old files cannot be checked. Source IDs do not establish handedness or participant identity.

Joint curves and frame data cover entry minus 1 s through exit plus 1 s, cut short at the next formal entry or end of recording. The preceding window can contain a previous trial's exit; see trial_window_coverage.csv. Curves use norm of 3D velocity and norm of 3D vector acceleration; both are nonnegative and vector acceleration differs from logged signed rate of speed change. Derivatives are computed before window clipping; no interpolation or filtering is applied. The timestamps are application receipt times, not verified hardware capture times.
