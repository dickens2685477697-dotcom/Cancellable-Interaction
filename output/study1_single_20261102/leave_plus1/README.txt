Study1, 20 successful trials. Entry aligned to 0; exit marked with a circle for each trial.
Each window begins at entry minus 1 s and ends at exit plus 1 s, earlier next formal entry, or end of recording, whichever comes first.
All original samples in each window are retained, including any unlabelled contact; physical re-entry or source changes need separate review.
The previous trial may overlap the beginning of the pre-entry window (notably trial 18).
Speed is the magnitude of the three-dimensional velocity. Acceleration magnitude is norm((v_i - v_(i-1))/dt_i).
Computations use consecutive finite valid positions from the same source and epoch, with 0 < dt <= 0.05 s. They are computed before window clipping.
The timestamp is the MRTK application-received timestamp, not a verified sensor capture timestamp. No interpolation, filtering or outlier removal.
End times and truncation are in trial_window_coverage.csv. Four metacarpal joints have no valid samples and are kept blank in the data.
